# Validation plan (PLANNED unless marked done; nothing here is customer-validated)
**Problem hypothesis:** custom-hamper sellers lose orders/margin from slow or error-prone quoting during peak. **WTP hypothesis:** at least 30% of demoed sellers will pay ₹999 up front (the rate assumed in first_five_customers.md).
**Done in assignment:** synthetic prototype tests only (9 logic + 5 UI checks). Not customer validation.
**Planned experiments (post-assignment):**
1. Problem interviews: 10 sellers, ask for last 3 inquiries and time/outcome (no pitching). Pass: ≥6 report losing/delaying orders on quoting.
2. Pre-order landing page + WhatsApp "QUOTE" CTA. Metric: click→message rate, message→demo rate.
3. Demo with seller's own inquiry; offer every seller the same ₹999, 60-day package before setup. Primary: paid ÷ demos. For each non-buyer, record one structured primary rejection reason (price / no pain / free tools are enough / bad timing) and a short verbatim explanation. Treat this as diagnostic evidence, not a price comparison. Consider a lower-price test only if most rejections cite price; those stated reasons alone do not prove a lower price would convert.
4. Usability test: seller completes quote in <5 minutes unaided (≥4 of 5).
**Guardrails:** refunds 0; setup ≤45 min; no more than 40% no-shows.
**Continue:** ≥5 paid, ≥3 used on a real inquiry. **Iterate:** 2–4 paid or strong interest without payment → review recorded rejection reasons and test an alternative offer only if the evidence points to price or service scope. **Pivot:** pain confirmed but sellers want done-for-you quoting or other pain (e.g., order tracking). **Stop:** ≤1 paid from 283 contacts, or ≥6 of 10 interviewees say existing free tools suffice.
**Invalidating evidence:** sellers quote in <2 minutes today; Meta's free agent already handles quoting; they prefer ₹0.
