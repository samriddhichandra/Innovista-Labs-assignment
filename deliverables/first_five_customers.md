# First five paying customers — plan (no outreach done; all numbers are editable assumptions)
**Segment & qualification:** Instagram/WhatsApp seller of custom hampers; handles ≥4 custom inquiries/month (ask); takes orders ≥10 units; does own quoting; not a large brand with a sales team.
**Channels (free):** (1) Instagram: find sellers via hashtags/location, comment/DM with a useful quote-checklist (permission-based, no spam); (2) seller/home-baker WhatsApp or Facebook communities, via admin permission; (3) Own reels/carousel (asset A). **Fallback:** community partnerships (asset B), then warm referrals from first users.
**Interest creator:** free "quote mistake checklist" + 60-second demo clip. **Landing conversion event:** WhatsApp message "QUOTE" or form submit with handle and monthly custom inquiries.
**Sales process:** lead → qualify (3 questions) → 15-min demo with their own real inquiry text → ₹999 payment link → 24h setup.
**Onboarding/delivery:** collect item prices (photo of price list OK) → I enter ≤40 items → test quote together → 14-day check-in.
